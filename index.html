Our focus: NDI®

From a cable for every signal to one network for all of them.

With HDMI and SDI, every camera needs its own cable to the switcher, and every extra screen needs another run. With NDI®, sources and displays join one Ethernet network, so any feed can reach any room.

Houses of worship

Churches are leaving the cable runs behind.

Many of our projects are churches that used to run HDMI and SDI and have moved to NDI®. Services stream with fewer cables, PTZ cameras run on a single Ethernet line, and the same feeds reach the main hall, overflow rooms and lobby screens.

  • One cable per camera

    With PoE, a single Ethernet run carries video, PTZ control, tally and power.

  • Every room gets the service

    Overflow halls, children’s rooms and lobby screens pick up any feed from the network.

  • Simple for volunteers

    One switcher, saved camera presets and a setup that works the same way every week.

How NDI® works

A typical church NDI® system

  1. 1

    Capture

    Telycam Explore NDI® PTZ cameras on PoE, plus the lyrics and slides PC sending NDI® from software.

  2. 2

    Produce

    Telycam Mix One or Magewell Director Plus switches the service, adds graphics and records.

  3. 3

    Distribute

    Magewell Pro Convert or CNDLive NDI Go decoders feed screens in every room.

  4. 4

    Stream

    The program goes live to YouTube or Facebook straight from the switcher.

NDI® solutions

Live video over a network, not a tangle of cables.

NDI® (Network Device Interface) sends video, audio and control over standard Ethernet. It has been Satu Visual’s focus for years: in the products we carry, the systems we design and the talks we give.

What changes when you switch

Fewer, simpler cables

One Ethernet cable can carry video, audio, PTZ control and tally, and power the camera with PoE.

Any source, anywhere

Every NDI® source on the network can be picked up in any room, switcher or software.

Grows with you

Add a camera or a screen by plugging it into the network instead of pulling new cable.

Keeps your current gear

Encoders bring existing HDMI and SDI cameras onto NDI®; decoders feed any display.

How we move you to NDI®

  1. 1

    Audit

    We map your cameras, cable runs, screens and stream as they are today.

  2. 2

    Network design

    We size the switch, bandwidth and PoE budget for your sources and rooms.

  3. 3

    Phased switch-over

    Existing gear keeps working through NDI® encoders while you replace in stages.

  4. 4

    Training and support

    We train your operators and volunteers, then support the system locally.
